Set up your open house page

You do this once per property. After that, every change in the property spreadsheet appears on the page automatically, and the QR sign never changes.

1. Turn on sharing

  1. Open your copy of the property spreadsheet.
  2. Click Share → under General access choose Anyone with the link → keep Viewer.
  3. Click Copy link.

Anyone with the page link can open this spreadsheet. Never put visitor names, emails or phone numbers in any of its tabs.

2. Make your sign-in form

  1. Open the sign-in form link in your PDF and click Make a copy. Keep the consent question, and in its text replace [Agent name] with your name exactly as in agent_name on the Basics tab: "By signing in, you agree that [Agent name] may contact you about this property by phone, text or email. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts." Your property page and QR sign fill in your name automatically. This wording is a starting point, not legal advice: check the rules for calls, texts and emails where you work (for example TCPA in the US, PECR/GDPR in the UK and EU).
  2. Upload Sign_In_Tracker.xlsx to Drive, open it and click File → Save as Google Sheets; delete its sample Form Responses 1 tab. Then in your form copy: Responses → Link to Sheets → Select existing spreadsheet → that copy (or Create a new spreadsheet, without the summary). Keep it private: it holds your visitors' details.
  3. Click Send → the link icon → Copy, and paste it into signin_link on the Basics tab.

Never connect the form to the property spreadsheet. The check below looks for this.
